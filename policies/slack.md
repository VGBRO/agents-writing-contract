# Slack Writing Policy

Route here from AGENTS.md when writing Slack messages.

## Limits

Parent post
  200 words or fewer.
  One paragraph per topic.
  Two topics at most.

Thread reply
  One paragraph. No headings.

## Structure

- Lead with the result or decision — not with context.
- Put context in a thread reply if a reader needs it to act.
- One link per post. More than one signals the post is doing too much.

## What to avoid

- Headings in a Slack post. They signal a document, not a message.
- Bullet lists longer than four items. Collapse them into a sentence or a linked doc.
- "Please see the above" or "as mentioned" — repeat the point in one clause instead.
